# SA Energy Market Demo

Public, standalone demonstration of the SA Energy Market Platform concept.

This repository contains only the static demo. The main product repository remains private.

## Live demo

Once GitHub Pages is enabled, the expected URL is:

https://xobho.github.io/sa-energy-market-demo/

## What the demo shows

- Fictional participant: ABC Platinum Mine
- Hourly demand profile
- Existing contracted supply
- Own generation
- Open energy position
- Illustrative seller stack
- Recommended BUY bid
- Simplified simulated market clearing
- Simulated market clearing price and market cost

## Important

This is an independent research prototype. It is not affiliated with NTCSA, NERSA, the Energy Council of South Africa, or the SAWEM Market Operator. It does not represent the final SAWEM OpenAPI schema, official clearing algorithm, or regulated settlement process.
